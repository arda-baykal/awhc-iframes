Core Entities

The five top-level records around which all AWHC data is organized.
5 terms
Person
An individual engaged by AWHC. Includes staff, board members, partner contacts, elders, lived-experience experts, and government officials.
Organization
A partner, funder, government body, service provider, or coalition that AWHC works with. Distinct from the people inside it.
Event
A point-in-time interaction logged in the system. Funder attribution lives on the Event level through Funder Category and Specific Funder.
A May 13 FNS training session in Calgary with 18 registered attendees.
Case
An ongoing engagement with a partner organization. A Case groups multiple related Events into a single relationship record. Status is either Active or Closed.
Program
A structured offering AWHC delivers. Each Program is tagged with one of five Activity Streams from the Logic Model.
Training and Technical Assistance, MtS Prevention Awards, Community of Practice.

Taxonomy & Types

The classification layers that describe what kind of Event or Case a record is.
5 terms
Event Category
The broadest grouping of an Event. Determines which Event Types and Activity Types are available for selection.
Training Activities, Community of Practice, Research & Knowledge Products.
Event Type
The second level of Event classification, scoped to a Category. Describes the format of the engagement.
CoP Call, Peer Knowledge Exchange, Webinar, Learning Exchange (under Community of Practice).
Activity Type
The third level — identifies the specific curriculum or stream delivered. Applies to Training, Community of Practice, and Research and Knowledge Products. Cascades from Event Category.
Housing First for Youth, Family and Natural Supports, Indigenous Homelessness Training, Foundations 101, Prevention.
Case Type
The form of an engagement journey. Distinct from Program and Funder; describes the relationship’s shape.
Coaching, Capacity Building, Policy Engagement, Coalition Building.
Activity Stream Logic Model
One of the five outcome pillars in AWHC’s Theory of Change: Influence, Research & Knowledge, Partnerships, Planning, and Practice. Every Program is tagged with one.

Funding

How AWHC tracks who pays for each Event for funder reporting.
2 terms
Funder Category
The three-bucket model for attributing Event funding. Drives the dashboard’s funding-attribution view.
Government of Canada, Philanthropic, Fee for Service.
Specific Funder
The free-text name of the actual funding source behind an Event. Captured alongside Funder Category for granular reporting.
ESDC, Home Depot Canada Foundation, Calgary Homeless Foundation.
monitoring

Reporting Concepts

The two layers of impact AWHC reports against the Logic Model.
2 terms
Outputs
Quantitative deliverables computed automatically from Event records.
Count of trainings delivered, partners engaged, Community of Practice calls held, policy briefs published.
Outcomes
Qualitative and quantitative results captured at the Event level through post-event surveys. Results aggregate by Activity Type and Program for funder reporting.
settings

System Conventions

Patterns the platform follows so the data stays clean over time.
2 terms
Active vs Closed
Records carry a lifecycle flag rather than being deleted. Closed records remain searchable and reportable, but are hidden from default views.
Hide, Don’t Delete
A historical record (an Event that happened, an Organization no longer engaged) is preserved with Active = false rather than removed — so historical reports stay reproducible.
